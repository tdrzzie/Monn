How to read these experiments

Each proposal is tested with its original stop and proposed target, a 1R target, and a 3R target. 1R is the initial planned stop risk. Paper sizing uses the existing monetary ceiling and lot caps. Simulations do not enforce live account loss or profitable-addition gates; blocked proposals remain observable.

Stop-first handling is used when both levels appear inside one bar. Quotes are sampled, historical short spread and conversion are approximations, and a 24-hour horizon is checked on eligible completed bars. Compare closed rows within the same strategy, version and cohort; seeded and live observations can overlap. No variant is automatically promoted to live trading.

01

02

Every same-direction position from that strategy must be profitable after swap and known opening fees before another can be added.

03

No numerical position-count cap. Every entry must still fit the monetary and loss limits.

04

Server-side stops are mandatory. Stops cannot be widened or removed. Other strategies’ and manual positions stay isolated.
